Service — 05 · Expert Assessment

Technical Expert
Assessment

Assessment of the technical condition of existing buildings, design documentation audits, and preparation for state expert review.

When Is Technical Expert Assessment Needed?

Before any significant intervention in an existing building, it is necessary to know its real technical condition. The expert assessment shows what load the building can bear, which elements need to be reinforced, and whether the planned change is possible at all.

  • Adding floors and reconstruction
  • Intervention in load-bearing structures
  • Change of the building's designated use
  • Investigation of cracks and deformations
  • Pre-sale assessment
  • Restoration of long-idle construction

Inspection Process

The inspection begins with a review of the documentation — the existing project, execution documents, and operating history. On site, the foundation, load-bearing walls, frame elements, partitions, floor and roof structures, as well as engineering utilities, are then inspected. Defects found — cracks, corrosion, dampness, settlement traces — are recorded and classified by nature.

Technical Report

The result of the inspection is an official technical report. The report reflects the current condition of the structures, the cause and hazard level of the defects found, conclusions on the building's load-bearing capacity, and photographic materials. The document serves as the basis for subsequent design or repair works.

Recommendations and Reinforcement

The report does not stop at merely noting the problem — a specific solution is proposed for every defect identified: reinforcing the structure, replacing the element, waterproofing, or reconstruction. Where necessary, separate design documentation is prepared for the reinforcement works.

Design Documentation Audit

The expert assessment service also includes reviewing design documentation prepared by another organization. The documents are reviewed for compliance with regulatory requirements, consistency between sections, and the accuracy of the cost estimate figures — this makes it possible to resolve any deficiencies before submission for state expert review.
