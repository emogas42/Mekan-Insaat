Civil Construction
Design

Design of high-rise residential buildings, private houses, and public facilities — from initial site investigation to state expert review.

Design Is Where Construction Begins

The future quality, safety, and operating costs of a building are determined precisely by the decisions made at the design stage. Our company offers solutions that ensure the functionality, structural stability, and energy efficiency of the building to be constructed; we prepare a complete, detailed description of the facility, aligning every detail with the client's requirements.

During the execution of the project, the client's technical brief, current construction norms and technical regulation requirements, as well as the specific conditions of the construction site, are all taken into account. The result is a unified set of documents on which the entire construction process is based — the working project.

Data Collection

Work on the project begins with an initial investigation. The results of the geotechnical survey provide information on soil composition and groundwater levels — the foundation type is selected precisely on the basis of this report. The topographic plan reflects the site's terrain, red lines, surrounding structures, underground utilities, and green areas. The technical specifications, in turn, address energy consumption issues and determine the connection points to the city's utility networks.

Concept Design

The design of modern buildings and structures continues with the preparation of initial solutions. The concept design consists of floor plans, sections, facades, and perspective renderings. At this stage the client gains a complete picture of the building's functional layout, area indicators, and architectural presence on the site. This is precisely where revisions are made — with the least time and cost.

Working Project

Structural solutions depend on technical and economic indicators. The design of the structures is based on the specific construction conditions and the materials selected; the goal is to ensure the required strength without increasing labor intensity or construction costs. The possibility of using modern construction materials is taken into account during the design of the buildings. The cost of construction and installation works is calculated on the basis of the working documents, and a cost estimate is prepared.

The completed project documentation consists of several volumes, including dedicated sections on fire safety, environmental protection, insolation, and measures to ensure the building's thermal insulation.

Composition of the Project Documentation

  • Architectural and construction section
  • Master plan and traffic solutions
  • Engineering sections
  • Technology section
  • Fire safety measures
  • Civil defense and emergency situations
  • Systems design
  • Environmental protection
  • Construction organization project
  • Cost estimate documentation
