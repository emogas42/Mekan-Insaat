Process

Our Work Process

01
Inquiry
The client gets in touch. We listen to the requirements and gather initial information about the project.
02
Contract
The technical brief is prepared. Terms are agreed and the contract is signed.
03
Preparation
A concept sketch is prepared. It is agreed with the client and revised as needed.
04
Working Design
The full documentation package is prepared. All technical solutions are finalized.
05
Handover
Documents are formally handed over to the client. Support is provided during expert review.
