About the Project

Multi-Storey Apart-Hotel Complex

The complete architectural and structural design for this apart-hotel complex on Abbasgulu Abbaszade St. in Baku's Sabail District was prepared by Məkan İnşaat MMC. With its multi-storey layout, the building combines both guest rooms and apart units intended for long-term rental.

The architectural solution was developed in full compliance with modern hotel standards. Facade design, interior layout, technical engineering systems, and structural solutions were all designed as an integrated whole by Məkan İnşaat MMC. The building's position on a major street in Sabail District was treated as a key urban-planning requirement from the design stage onward.
