Privacy Policy

Rules governing the collection, processing, protection, and use of your personal data.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Dear user, welcome to the website www.mekan-inshaat.az (hereinafter — the "Website"). This Privacy Policy governs the rules for the collection, processing, protection, and use of users' personal data by "Məkan İnşaat" MMC (hereinafter — the "Company").

By using our website or filling out any of the forms, you accept the terms of this Privacy Policy and consent to the processing of your personal data in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Azerbaijan.

1. Personal Data We Collect

When you fill out an application, contact, or vacancy form on our Website, you voluntarily provide us with the following information:

  • Identification data: your first name, last name, and patronymic.
  • Contact information: your phone number, email address.
  • Professional information (for job applications): your CV (resume), work experience, education details, and any other documents you submit.
  • Automatically collected data: when you access the Website, certain technical information may be collected, including your IP address, browser type, session duration, and data gathered via cookies.

2. Purposes of Collecting and Processing Data

The personal data we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries regarding our design and construction services and to stay in contact with you;
  • To evaluate CVs submitted through our recruitment (vacancy) forms and to contact suitable candidates;
  • To send you news, offers, and information about our services (you may opt out of this at any time);
  • To improve the functioning of the Website and to ensure its security.

3. Disclosure of Personal Data to Third Parties

Our Company strictly observes the confidentiality of users' personal data. Your personal data is never sold, rented, or transferred to third parties, except in the following cases:

  • In cases directly provided for by the legislation of the Republic of Azerbaijan (on the basis of an official request from a court or law enforcement authority);
  • When you have given your direct consent in written or electronic form.

4. Data Security and Protection

We use modern technical and organizational security measures (such as the SSL encryption protocol) to prevent the loss, unlawful use, alteration, or unauthorized access of your personal data. However, it is not possible to guarantee 100% security for any data transmitted over the internet.

5. User Rights

In accordance with the Law of the Republic of Azerbaijan "On Personal Data," you have the following rights:

  • To obtain information about the personal data we hold about you;
  • To request the correction of inaccurate or outdated data;
  • To request the complete deletion of your personal data from our systems (the right to be forgotten);
  • To withdraw your consent to the processing of your data.

To exercise these rights, you may contact us at info@mekan-inshaat.az.

6. Changes to the Privacy Policy

The Company reserves the right to amend this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes take effect from the moment they are published on the Website. By continuing to use the Website, you acknowledge that you have reviewed the updates.
